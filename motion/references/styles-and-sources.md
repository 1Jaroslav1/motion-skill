## Selected tutorial demo: six parts

Based on the user's choice handed over on 16.10.2026. Format and duration come from the demo's separate brief; they are not set here.

| Part | Visual language and action |
|---|---|
| Hand-drawn pencil sketch | Drawn stroke, pressure/irregularity of line, construction of forms, light paper texture. Text/drawing appears along a meaningful pencil path; a plain outline of a printed font is not handwriting. Deliberate swapping of drawings is fine with a smooth controlled camera. |
| Paper | Cut-out shapes, layers, edges/contact shadows, folds and plasticity of the material. Stretch, squash, collision and local stop-motion stepping support the action rather than replace it with random jitter. |
| Pixel | One working pixel grid, consistent scales of shapes and glyphs, controlled motion without accidental blurring of edges. Separate the rate of drawn states from the output fps; final text is readable. |
| 2D + SaaS/UI | Flat interface with cards, panels, buttons, a clear cursor and causal state changes. The viewer sees the product working through a concrete action. |
| 2.5D + SaaS/UI | The same interface: flat panels separated in depth; the camera reveals layers and parallax. Don't fake real 3D thickness or sides. |
| 3D + SaaS/UI | The same recognisable objects gain real thickness, edges and side faces, matching light/shadows, volume and a spatial orbit/fly-through. The difference from 2.5D is visible in the output. |

In the last three parts preserve the identity of the interface, data and objects; change only the way of spatial representation. Interaction and projection rules are in [SaaS/UI motion](saas-ui-motion.md).

Across all six parts use one fixed screen-space indicator: constant position and styling, only the name of the current part changes. Don't attach it to the world camera; protect it from overlap and cropping. Tie energetic transitions to expressive accents of the actual music/SFX while keeping reading and meaning. This is labelling and direction for the tutorial demo, not a universal requirement for other videos.
