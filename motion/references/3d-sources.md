# Sources of ready-made 3D models

Optional module: read when the brief needs ready-made models. Based on research handed over for this edition on 16.10.2026; that does not mean the author of this edit downloaded and tested every file. Terms and availability of a given release may change: check its page and the bundled licence at download time. The order below is a choice by task, not a mandatory chain of sites.

| Task and source | What to consider |
|---|---|
| Realistic objects — [Poly Haven Models](https://polyhaven.com/models) | glTF, CC0, no registration. Good for realistic props; check the texture set and the actual model. |
| Stylised packs — [Kenney Assets](https://kenney.nl/assets) | Pick a 3D pack specifically: GLB available, CC0. "Continue without donating" gets the free pack without a mandatory donation. |
| Characters and rigs — [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/) | Free Standard releases often include FBX/glTF; Source may be paid. Keep the licence of the exact release you chose; don't treat the whole site as CC0. See the note below. |
| Real 3D scans — [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/list?type=3d-model,terrain) | CC0; check the format and ZIP contents. Don't promise that every object is available as GLB. |
| Specific devices and other models — [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/) | Look for downloadable/free, glTF/GLB and the terms of the specific model. Downloading needs an account; use existing access or another source, never self-register. |
| Free models with a format filter — [CGTrader](https://www.cgtrader.com/3d-models?free=1) | Check the glTF filter, a genuinely zero price and the individual terms. "Free in subscription" is not a free download; waits and limits are possible. |
| Stylised characters — [KayKit Adventurers](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers) | Free part: 5 models, FBX/glTF, CC0; extras are paid. Don't attribute the extra content to the free part. |
| Technical fallback — [Khronos glTF Sample Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets) | GLB/glTF for compatibility and technical scenes. The licence is in each asset's README; don't substitute the repository-wide licence for it. |

**Quaternius:** [QAL v1.0 of 28.08.2026](https://quaternius.com/license.html) allows personal and commercial finished projects but forbids redistributing standalone assets, packs and templates. Some older specific releases may remain CC0. Don't transfer the licence of one release to another. In this skill package keep the information and links; never bundle the model itself inside the distributed skill.

**Poly Pizza:** don't use it as the primary automated source: its [terms](https://poly.pizza/docs/tos) restrict automated downloads. This limits the acquisition method, not 3D in the project. Pick another suitable free source; don't bypass the access restriction.

**Network note:** in sandboxed environments shell downloads (curl/wget, scripts) to most of these sites are often blocked by a network allowlist. Don't route around the block; use an allowed fetch tool if it can retrieve the file, pick a reachable source (e.g. the Khronos GitHub repository), or ask the user to attach the model.

A normal permitted download for a personal video does not require abandoning the project over possible future commercial use. At the end of the result briefly state the source, author, release/version and terms actually used. If a model is unavailable, paid, or its terms don't fit the specific action, find the next one; never treat "free" as permission for every kind of distribution. Model preparation is described in the [3D process](models-3d.md).
